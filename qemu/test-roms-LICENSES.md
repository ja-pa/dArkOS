# Free validation ROMs

The NES, Game Boy and Game Boy Advance suites are by Damian Yerrick, version
v0.23: https://github.com/pinobatch/240p-test-mini/tree/v0.23

The SNES 1.10 and Mega Drive 1.32 suites are by Artemio Urbina and contributors:
https://github.com/ArtemioUrbina/240pTestSuite
https://artemiourbina.itch.io/240p-test-suite

All are GPL-2.0-or-later. Preserve the upstream license/readme and source links
when redistributing. Exact downloaded artifacts are recorded in test-roms.json.
These tests check frontend/core integration, not timing accuracy or playability.
